Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

For the safety of all pets, we require up-to-date documentation for Rabies, DHPP (Distemper/Parvo), and Bordetella. You can upload vaccination records directly into our online portal before your visit.
Self-service washtubs operate primarily on a first-come, first-served basis, but you can also reserve a tub time online to guarantee zero waiting during busy weekend hours!
Most full grooming services take approximately 2 to 3.5 hours depending on coat size, condition, and pet behavior. We send a text notification as soon as your pup is ready for pickup!
We request at least 24 hours notice for grooming and boarding cancellations so we can offer the appointment spot to clients on our waiting list.
